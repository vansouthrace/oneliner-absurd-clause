Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1): one original English sentence, up to 200 characters, phrased as an absurd clause buried deep in a terms-of-service agreement.

## The entry

> Section 8.3: the Company reserves the right to attend the user's wedding as a plus-one, and to invoice the user for its own gift.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The clause reads as pure deadpan boilerplate until the second twist lands: the Company doesn't just crash your wedding, it invoices you for its own gift. It beats the field's NPC, dreams-license, and soul-in-escrow clauses because the absurdity is concrete and double-layered — a plus-one no one invited, charging you for the privilege — and it uses none of the field's overworked tropes.
